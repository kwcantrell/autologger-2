# Panel: catalog-retry-backoff
Tier: 2 · Reviewers: assumption tester, failure and abuse, scope and simplicity · Date: 2026-10-02

No critical findings. The assumption tester re-ran the probe and reproduced it: jitter with 5 runs gave 0/240 with 8 writers, plus 0/2400 with 8, 0/600 with 12 and 0/800 with 16 in a larger stress run. The spec delta is verbatim apart from the intended edits. The KV store and the mirror use root statements, not `tx`, so they are unaffected. Commit-unknown is still never retried, and every `.tx(` body found is database-only.

- [x] [major] A re-run that starts after the deadline is not refused before it starts. `acquire()` hands out a free slot whatever the deadline, and `attempt()` sends `BEGIN` under an about 1 ms bounded timer, which retires a connection. With a fake connection, the run even commits. D2's claim "the next attempt raises the existing timeout error" was false. (Found by two reviewers.) Evidence: fake-connection probe `attempt(body, Date.now() - 5)` -> `"out": ["COMMITTED","COMMITTED"]`; `txSlots:1, txTimeoutMs:0` probe -> `statements sent: ["BEGIN ISOLATION LEVEL SERIALIZABLE"] | new connections: 1`; `sed -n 468,472p postgresCatalogStore.ts` -> no deadline check in `acquire`. Resolved: D2 adds a deadline check before every re-run that throws `CatalogTxTimeoutError` with no statement or connection; there is a new spec scenario and a 1.1 unit case.
- [x] [major] Docs that state "3 runs" were missing from Impact and the tasks: `packages/ports/src/catalogDb.ts:13` and `docs/supabase.md:149`. Evidence: `grep -rniE "three (runs|tries)|3 (runs|tries)|at most 3" --exclude-dir=node_modules --exclude-dir=archive .` -> both lines. Resolved: both are in proposal Impact and task 3.1.
- [x] [major] Task 3.1 didn't name every ADR 0021 retry-exhaustion line (hazard 20 at line 182, the flood item at 280, the session-create item at 286-288), and its verify grep couldn't catch a leftover "3". Evidence: `grep -n "exhaust" docs/decisions/0021-migrate-to-self-hosted-supabase.md` -> 182, 280, 286. Resolved: 3.1 names each line, and its verify step greps for the old wording.
- [x] [minor] Nothing required the wait to hold no connection; putting it inside `attempt` would starve the 5 slots. Evidence: probe `txSlots:1` -> `free slots after reject = 1` (safe today). Resolved: the spec bullet says it holds no connection; D2 and a 1.1 unit case assert every slot is free during a wait.
- [x] [minor] Raising 3 to 5 runs means up to 5/3 the database work per contended request under a deliberate flood (team writes have no rate limit; the invite path scans `users`). Evidence: `grep -n "SELECT \* FROM users" packages/catalog/src/authStore.ts` -> `493`. Resolved: accepted in the proposal's Risks section; the rate-limit and email-index items stay on the ADR revisit list (3.1).
- [x] [minor] The 8-writer scenario states a probabilistic outcome as certain, and its "randomized wait" clause can't be observed in a pg test; 10 repetitions under CI load could flake. Evidence: design Risks "A probe on a quiet machine". Resolved: the clause moved out of the THEN (the requirement bullet and the unit test cover the wait); 5 repetitions; the test reports run counts on failure; the risk is recorded in the proposal.
- [x] [minor] The bounds disagreed: closed in the proposal, half-open in the design, "between" in the spec. Resolved: half-open `[0, 20 × 2^(n−1))` everywhere.
- [x] [minor] Closing during a wait was underspecified, and the task wording ("no wait follows close()") contradicted D2. Evidence: `sed -n 285,290p postgresCatalogStore.ts` -> `await Promise.allSettled([...this.running])`. Resolved: D2 checks close both before the wait (no wait starts once closed) and after it (no new run); `close()` waits at most the remaining backoff; 1.1 is reworded.
- [x] [minor] The unit test needs control of `Date.now` for the deadline cases. Evidence: `const deadlineAt = Date.now() + this.txTimeoutMs;`. Resolved: D3 uses `vi.useFakeTimers({ toFake: ['Date'] })`, with the injected `sleep` advancing it.
- [ ] [minor] A forward jump in the wall clock (`Date.now`) can end a transaction early. This predates the change; `performance.now()` would avoid it. Not in scope.
- [x] [minor] The injectable `random` and `sleep` are worth keeping: they follow the existing `connect` injection, and the unit and pg tests complement each other. No change.
