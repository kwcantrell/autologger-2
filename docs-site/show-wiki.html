The show wiki

Each show has a wiki: “show memory for producers”. It holds pages about people, topics, running bits and each session, written in plain text with formatting, with full history. AI can suggest updates from a session's transcript for you to review. Used by members and producers who need to remember who said what across episodes.

How to open the wiki

  1. Choose the show in the top bar.
  2. Click Wiki in the top bar.
  3. Browse the tabs People, Topics, Running bits, Sessions and Deleted, or type in Filter by title or alias.

How to create a page

  1. Click New page.
  2. Choose the Kind: Person, Topic or Running bit.
  3. Type the Title and click Create. If that title is taken, click Open that page instead.

From a session, click Wiki page beside the tabs to open (or create) that session's page.

How to edit a page

  1. Open the page and click Edit.
  2. Change the Title, add Aliases (type one and press Enter or click Add), and edit the text in Write. Check it in Preview.
  3. Click Save.

Pages support formatting such as headings, lists and links. Citations link a quote to the moment in the session where it was said; clicking one opens the session at that point.

How to get AI suggestions from a session

  1. Open a session that has a transcript.
  2. Click Update wiki beside the tabs. It shows Updating wiki… while it runs.
  3. When suggestions are ready, a message says how many; click Review.
  4. In the wiki's Suggestions list, click one to open Review suggestion. Look at Changes or Preview.
  5. Click Accept, Edit, then accept (then Accept with edits), or Dismiss.

A page with a waiting suggestion shows “A suggested update is waiting for review” with a Review suggestion button.

How to see history and restore an old version

  1. On a page, click History.
  2. Choose a revision to see what changed compared with the current page. Click Load older for earlier ones.
  3. Click Restore rN (where N is the revision number) to bring it back as a new revision.

How to merge, split, delete and restore pages

  • Merge into…: choose the page that takes this one in. Its text is added to the end of the other page, its title and aliases become aliases there, and it then redirects. History keeps both pages.
  • Split: cut text from Stays on this page, paste it into Moves to the new page, choose the New page kind and New page title, then click Split.
  • Delete: confirm Delete page. The page leaves the wiki and frees its title, but its history is kept. Restore it from the Deleted tab with Restore.

Permissions and requirements

  • Anyone who can open a show can read and edit its wiki.
  • Update wiki uses your own language model (Settings → Advanced).

Common problems

  • “No show chosen”. Choose a show in the top bar.
  • “This page changed since you opened it.” Someone else saved first. Your text is still there; copy what you need, click Load the newer page, and edit again.
  • “No suggestions this time”. The AI found nothing new to add.
  • “This page belongs to…”. You opened a page of another show. Click Switch to that show to work in it.
