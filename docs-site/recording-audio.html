Recording audio

Members can record a session's audio straight from the browser while timecode rolls. AutoLogger saves the recording in short pieces as it goes, so little is lost if the connection or browser fails. This page is for the operator who records.

How to record

  1. Open the session and click Roll timecode.
  2. Click Record audio (the microphone button).
  3. If your browser asks for microphone access, allow it.
  4. The status shows REC, with a Microphone level meter and the recording's running time. An internal “Recording 1 Started” event is logged.
  5. To stop recording but keep timecode rolling, click Stop recording audio. To stop both, click Stop timecode.
  6. While the last audio is saved you see Saving Audio.... Wait for it to finish before closing the tab.

Tips while recording

  • Keep the tab visible. Browsers may slow down or pause a hidden tab. If the tab was in the background, AutoLogger tells you how long, so you can check the audio.
  • Your screen stays awake while recording on devices that allow it.
  • If the microphone disconnects, AutoLogger tries to pick it up again and carries on. If it cannot, recording stops and what was captured is saved.
  • Leaving the page while recording asks you to confirm, because you could lose the latest audio.

How to rescue audio that did not upload

If some recorded pieces could not be uploaded, a banner appears above the workspace: “N recorded audio chunks could not be uploaded. Keep this tab open until they are retried, downloaded, or discarded.”

  1. Click Retry all (or Retry on one piece) to try again.
  2. If you were signed out, sign in again in another tab, then press Retry.
  3. Click Download to save a piece to your computer as a backup.
  4. Only if you are sure you do not need it, click Discard (or Discard remaining) and confirm. This cannot be undone.

If the browser closed during a recording, the next time you open AutoLogger in that browser it offers the recovered audio with Upload all, Download all and Discard recovered. Some browsers (Safari) keep unsent recordings for only 7 days without a visit.

Permissions

Anyone who can open the show can record. Only one browser tab, window or person can record a session at a time; while someone else records, your transport buttons are disabled.

Common problems

  • Record audio is greyed out. Roll timecode first. It is also disabled while another client records, and briefly shows “Recovering unsent audio…” while AutoLogger checks this browser for unsent recordings.
  • “Microphone access denied.” Allow the microphone in your browser's site settings and try again.
  • “Another window, tab, or user now holds the recording lease.” Someone else started recording this session; your recording has stopped.
