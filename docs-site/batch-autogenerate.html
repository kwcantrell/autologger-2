Batch autogenerate

Batch autogenerate fills in what is missing across many sessions in one background job: transcripts, auto-generated events, topics and wiki updates, oldest session first. It is for team owners and admins catching up an archive.

How to start a batch job

  1. Open Settings → Batch autogenerate (under your team).
  2. Choose the Team.
  3. Under Shows, tick All shows or the shows to include.
  4. Under Steps, tick Transcripts, Log events, Topics and/or Wiki.
  5. Under Wiki output, choose Add new pages and additions to the wiki or Leave all as suggestions to review.
  6. Read the estimate (for example “Run on 14 sessions, at most 40 runs.”).
  7. Click Start and confirm Start batch autogenerate.

How the job runs

  • Sessions run one at a time, oldest first, and each step runs only where its output is missing (a session that already has a transcript is not transcribed again).
  • Steps run in order; if one fails, the rest of that session is skipped and the job moves on.
  • The job runs on your provider accounts, keeps going if you close the browser, and resumes after a server restart.
  • If the provider fails on three sessions in a row, the job stops.

How to follow and cancel a job

  1. Under Recent jobs, each job shows Queued, Running, Finished, Cancelled or Stopped, and what it is doing now (“Now: session · step”).
  2. To stop it, click Cancel job and confirm. The job stops after its current step; work already done is kept.
  3. When it ends, the report lists each session and the result of each step.

Permissions and requirements

  • Only owners and admins of a team can run, view or cancel its batch jobs; the section is hidden from everyone else.
  • You need a speech-to-text key for Transcripts and a language model for the other steps (see AI and transcription accounts).
  • The server may limit this to approved users; ask your administrator if told so.

Common problems

  • “Stopped: the initiator has no speech-to-text key.” (or no language model) Add it and start again.
  • “Stopped: the initiator is no longer an owner or admin of this team.” The person who started the job lost their role.
  • “Pick at least one show and one step.” Tick a show and a step.
