Transcripts

The Transcript tab holds a word-by-word transcript of the session's audio, with speaker labels, lined up with the session timecode. Members generate it after recording or importing audio, then correct it by hand.

How to generate a transcript

  1. Make sure the session has audio (recorded or imported).
  2. Open the Transcript tab.
  3. Click Auto generate. The tab shows “Generating transcript… this may take a couple minutes.”
  4. When it finishes, rows appear with Session Time, Speaker and Word(s). The count shows the number of words.

Generating again replaces the whole transcript with a new one.

How to correct a transcript

  1. Click into a row's Session Time, Speaker or Word(s) field.
  2. Type the correction.
  3. Click or tab out of the field to save it.

To add a missing row, click Insert. To hear a word, click its jump button. If someone else changed the same row, the Row changed dialog lets you choose Overwrite or Keep theirs.

Only one transcription at a time

The server transcribes one session at a time. While another session is being transcribed, the toolbar shows “Transcribing “session name”…” with the time elapsed; click the name to open that session. Wait for it to finish, then generate yours.

Requirements and privacy

  • Transcription uses your own speech-to-text account (DeepGram). Add it in Settings → Advanced (see AI and transcription accounts). The session's audio is sent to DeepGram's cloud and billed to your key.
  • On some servers transcription is limited to approved users; ask your administrator if you are told so.

Common problems

  • “Transcription needs your own speech-to-text key.” Add a DeepGram key in Settings → Advanced. You can still add rows by hand with Insert.
  • “No transcript yet.” Click Auto generate.
  • Speakers are labelled oddly. Speaker labels come from the transcription service; correct them in the Speaker field.
