Timeline and playback

The timeline shows the whole session at a glance: audio waveform, recorded clips and a marker for each event. Members use it to listen back and to jump between moments.

How to play back audio

  1. Make sure timecode is stopped (status STOPPED).
  2. Click Play or pause audio, or press Space. The status shows PLAY.
  3. Click Pause audio or press Space again to pause.

How to move around the timeline

  1. Click or drag on the Timeline scrubber to move the playhead.
  2. With the playhead focused, press ← or → to move 1 second, or hold Shift to move 10 seconds.
  3. Use the Timeline zoom controls (Reduce timeline zoom / Increase timeline zoom) or press + and − to zoom in and out.
  4. Click Previous marker or Next marker to step between logged events. The timeline shows the Current marker. Marker navigation moves the playhead but does not start playback.

How to jump to a row from a feed

  1. In the Event Feed, Transcript or Topics tab, find the row.
  2. Click its jump button (named Jump to and the row's time).
  3. The timeline moves to that point and playback starts from there. If no recording covers that moment, only the playhead moves.

What the timeline shows

  • Clips for each recording or import. A gap labelled “No audio file for this log interval” means events were logged without audio.
  • Generating waveform… or Finalizing recording… while new audio is processed; Waveform unavailable if it could not be drawn. Playback still works without a waveform.
  • One marker per event, coloured by its event button.

Common problems

  • Jump buttons are unavailable. Jumping is turned off while timecode is rolling, while the session is still loading, or during batch edit.
  • A topic has no jump button. Topics can only be placed once the session has a transcript.
  • “Audio failed to load.” Reload the page; if the clip still fails, the audio may still be processing.
