Getting around AutoLogger

A quick tour of the screen every member sees after signing in: the left sidebar, the top bar, the home screen and Settings. Read this first if you are new; it names the controls the other pages refer to.

What you see

  • Left sidebar: New session, Import, a Search sessions… box, your Recent sessions, any Archived sessions, and Settings at the bottom with the app version beneath it.
  • Top bar: the sidebar toggle, then your current team › show › transport status (for example STOPPED and “No session open”), then Search and Wiki.
  • Home screen: the AutoLogger name, a card for your most recent session with Jump back in, and New session (or Start a session if you have none yet).

How to switch team or show

  1. In the top bar, click the team name (it shows No team if none is chosen).
  2. Under Your teams, pick a team. Each entry shows how many shows it has and your role (Owner, Admin or Member).
  3. Click the show name next to it and pick a show from Shows in your team.

Switching team closes the session you have open. The show you choose decides which event buttons, wiki and sessions you work with. If Settings is open with unsaved changes, AutoLogger asks before switching.

How to open, collapse and use the sidebar

  1. Press [ or Ctrl+B (⌘+B on a Mac), or click the sidebar toggle at the left of the top bar. When collapsed, the sidebar becomes a strip of icons with tooltips.
  2. Click a session under Recent sessions to open it. The card for the open session is marked ACTIVE SESSION; a session whose timecode is running somewhere is marked LIVE SESSION.
  3. Type in Search sessions… to filter the list by name. Press Esc or click Clear search to clear it. To search inside transcripts, topics and events, use the top bar's Search instead.

On a phone the sidebar opens as a drawer and closes once you choose something.

How to open and close Settings

  1. Click Settings at the bottom of the sidebar.
  2. Choose a section on the left. Sections are grouped as You (Account, Companion devices, Advanced), your team (Members, Shows, Team details, and Batch autogenerate for owners and admins) and your show (Show details, Event buttons).
  3. Make changes. Most changes open a side panel with Save and Cancel; nothing is saved until you click Save.
  4. Click Back to session (or Back to sessions), or press Esc, to return. If you have unsaved changes, AutoLogger asks Discard changes? — choose Keep editing to stay.

Permissions

New session and Import appear only when the current team has at least one show you can open. A session in a show you cannot open is listed but greyed, with “No access — ask a team admin”.
