Teams, roles and invitations

A team (also called a studio) groups people and shows. Owners and admins run the team: they invite members, set roles, decide which shows each member can open, and hand over or delete the team. Members can see who is on the team.

Each team has exactly one owner. Anyone can create a new team and becomes its owner.

How to invite someone

  1. Open Settings and choose Members under your team.
  2. In Invite someone, type their Email address (for example producer@example.com).
  3. Click Send invite.
  4. If they already use AutoLogger they join at once. Otherwise they appear as Invited (“Invited. Joins at first sign-in.”) and join as a member when they first sign in with that Google account.
  5. Give them show access (next section); new members start with no shows.

To cancel a pending invite, click Revoke next to it.

How to give a member access to shows

  1. In Members, click the member.
  2. Under Show access, tick the shows they may open and untick the rest.
  3. Click Save.

You can also choose who opens a show from the show itself: Shows → Edit → Who can open it. Removing access takes effect at once, and closes any session of that show the member has open.

How to change someone's role

  1. As the owner, open Members and click the person.
  2. Under Role, choose Admin or Member.
  3. Click Save.

How to remove someone from the team

  1. In Members, click the person.
  2. Under Membership, click Remove… (or Keep on team to undo).
  3. Click Remove member and confirm. They lose access right away, including to any open sessions.

How to rename the team or set its frame rate

  1. Open Settings → Team details.
  2. Change Team name or Default frame rate (new sessions in this team start at this rate). The Team id cannot be changed.
  3. Click Save.

How to hand over, leave or delete a team

  • Transfer ownership (owner): in Team details, click Transfer…, pick a member and confirm Transfer. You become an admin.
  • Leave team (admins and members): click Leave team and confirm. You need a new invite to rejoin. An owner must transfer ownership first.
  • Delete team (owner): click Delete team and confirm. A team that still has shows cannot be deleted. This cannot be undone.

How to create another team

In Team details, use Create a team: type a Team id (slug) and Display name and click Create team. You become its owner. Switch between teams from the top bar.

Common problems

  • Controls are greyed out. “Only owners and admins can invite people or change roles and show access.”
  • A member sees no sessions. They have no show access yet; give them shows.
  • “Discard changes?” You closed a panel without saving. Choose Keep editing and click Save.
