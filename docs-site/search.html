Searching across shows and sessions

Search finds studios (teams), shows, sessions and moments (transcript passages, topics and events) across every team you belong to. Producers use it to find any word or theme without re-listening. You only ever see results you are allowed to open.

How to search quickly

  1. Click Search in the top bar, or press Ctrl+K (⌘+K on a Mac).
  2. Type at least 2 characters in Search sessions, transcripts, topics, events….
  3. Use the arrow keys or mouse to pick a result:
    • a session opens it;
    • a moment opens its session at that point in the timeline;
    • a show or studio switches to it.
  4. Click See all results for the full results page.

How to use the results page

  1. Refine the query in the Search box.
  2. Narrow results with the studio filter (All studios) and show filter (All shows).
  3. Results are grouped as Studios, Shows, Sessions and Moments, with moments grouped by session. Click Open session or a moment to go there.

How to write a query

  • Case and accents are ignored, and word forms match (“budget” finds “budgets”).
  • Put a phrase in quotes to match it exactly: "lock the export".
  • Put a minus before a word to exclude it: budget -travel.
  • Use or for either word: budget or forecast.
  • Show and session names also match close misspellings and partial names.

Common problems

  • “No results for …”. Try fewer words or remove filters. Moments are only found once a session has a transcript, topics or events.
  • A session you expect is missing. You may not have access to its show; ask a team owner or admin.
  • “Search failed.” The search took too long or the server was busy; click Try again or narrow the query.
