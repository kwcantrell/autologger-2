Shows

A show groups the sessions of one programme or project, and carries its own event buttons, wiki and access list. Owners and admins create and set up shows; members work in the shows they can open.

How to add a show

  1. Open Settings → Shows under your team.
  2. Under Add a show, click Add show.
  3. In New show, type the Show name (it appears in the top bar and on every session).
  4. Type a Code, for example MD for Morning Desk. It starts the name of every untitled session; left empty, the name's initials are used.
  5. Choose the Suffix: Date or Episode Number. This decides how an untitled session's name ends.
  6. Under Who can open it, tick the members who may open the show. Owners and admins can always open every show.
  7. Click Add show.

How to change a show

  1. In Shows, click Edit next to the show (the current show is marked Current show), or open Show details for the active show.
  2. Change the name, code, suffix or who can open it.
  3. Click Save.

How to set up the show's event buttons

See Event buttons.

Permissions

Only owners and admins can add or change shows. Members see the show settings but cannot change them.

Common problems

  • “No shows yet”. The team has no shows. Add one, or ask an owner or admin.
  • A member cannot see the show. Tick them under Who can open it.
