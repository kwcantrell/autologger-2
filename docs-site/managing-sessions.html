Managing sessions

Renaming, archiving, restoring, deleting and sharing sessions. For any member who keeps the session list tidy or sends a colleague a link.

How to rename a session

  1. In the sidebar, hover over the session and click the Session options menu (the three dots).
  2. Choose Rename.
  3. Type the new Session name and click Save (or press Enter).

How to archive and restore a session

  1. Open Session options on the session and choose Archive.
  2. Confirm with Archive. The session moves to the Archived group in the sidebar.
  3. To bring it back, open its menu under Archived, choose Restore, and confirm. It returns to Recent sessions.

Opening a link to an archived session shows “This session is archived. Restore it to open the workspace.” with a Restore session button.

How to delete a session

  1. Open Session options (in Recent or Archived) and choose Delete.
  2. Confirm. The session, its events, audio, transcript and topics are deleted permanently. This cannot be undone.

How to close the open session

Open Session options on the active session and choose Close session. You return to the home screen. If your timecode was rolling, AutoLogger stops the timecode you started when you leave.

How to share a link to a session or a moment

  1. Open the session and copy the address from your browser's address bar.
  2. Send it to a colleague. When they open it (signing in first if needed) the session opens directly.
  3. Links that come from Search results and wiki citations can point to a moment; opening one moves the timeline to that point without starting playback.

Permissions

Session menus are only available on sessions in shows you can open. A link to a session you cannot open shows Session not found, the same as a link to a session that does not exist.

Common problems

  • “Session not found”. The session was deleted, the link is wrong, or you no longer have access to its show.
  • “Couldn't load session”. Check your connection and click Try again.
