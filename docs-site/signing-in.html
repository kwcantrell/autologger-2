Signing in and your account

Everyone who uses AutoLogger signs in with a Google account; there is no anonymous mode and no separate password. This page is for anyone opening AutoLogger for the first time, and for anyone who needs to update their name or sign out.

How to sign in

  1. Open the AutoLogger address your team gave you. If you are signed out, you see the AutoLogger sign-in card: “Sign in to open your sessions, markers, and transcripts.”
  2. Click Sign in with Google.
  3. Choose your Google account and approve the request on Google's page.
  4. You return to AutoLogger. If you opened a link to a particular session before signing in, AutoLogger takes you back to that session.

How to create an account

  1. On the sign-in card, under New to AutoLogger?, click Create an account with Google.
  2. Sign in with Google as above. Your account is created automatically the first time you sign in; there is no separate sign-up form.
  3. If someone invited your email address to a team, you join that team as a member right away. If not, you see Create your first team (see below).

Note: some servers only let in people who have been invited. If you see “This Google account doesn't have access yet”, ask the person who gave you the link to invite your email address, then try again.

How to create your first team

If you are signed in but not on any team, AutoLogger shows Create your first team instead of the normal screen.

  1. In Team id (slug), type a short id in lower case, for example my-crew. It cannot be changed later.
  2. In Display name, type the name people will see, for example My Crew.
  3. Click Create team. You become the team's owner and the normal workspace opens.

How to change your name or sign out

  1. Click Settings at the bottom of the left sidebar.
  2. Under You, choose Account.
  3. Edit First name and Last name, then click Save. Your Email is the Google account you sign in with and is shown for reference only.
  4. To leave, click Sign out. This ends your session on this device only.

Common problems

  • “This sign-in attempt expired.” The sign-in took too long or was opened in another tab. Click Try again.
  • “Sign-in was cancelled or refused.” You closed or declined the Google page. Click Try again.
  • “Sign-in didn't complete.” Something else went wrong. Try again; if it keeps happening, contact your administrator.
  • The page only shows a Try again button. AutoLogger could not reach the server to check whether you are signed in. Check your connection and click Try again.
  • You are signed out in the middle of work. The sign-in card replaces the workspace. Sign in again; if you were recording, see Recording audio for how unsent audio is kept.
