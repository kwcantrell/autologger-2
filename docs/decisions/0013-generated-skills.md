# 0013: Skills have one source; generated copies are never edited

- Date: 2026-09-29
- Status: Accepted
- Rule: check `skills-sync`; deny rules on `.agents/**` and `.claude/skills/openspec-*`

## Context

autologger-2 hand-patched OpenSpec's generated skills, and regeneration overwrote the patches.
infisical kept `.claude/` and `.agents/` skill copies that were synced by hand.

## Decision

`.claude/skills/` is the single source for the lifecycle's own skills. `scripts/sync-skills.sh`
generates `.agents/skills/` from it for Codex and other agents, and CI fails on drift. OpenSpec's
skills are generated by `openspec init`/`update` and are customized only through
`openspec/config.yaml` `rules:` and `operations:`.

## Evidence

The two incidents above.

## Consequences

A behavior that `rules:`/`operations:` can't express needs a wrapper skill of our own, not a
patch to OpenSpec's.
